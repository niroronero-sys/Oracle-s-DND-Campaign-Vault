A constantly evolving hivemind.

If the fight drags on, they are able to analyze and adapt to an attack, element, or weapon. And once they adapt, if they establish a link between other Sea Demons, then those SD's also adapt, and when they come into contact with either the central nervous system for Sea Demons or a Check point, then the entire species will now have adapted.
-
Classes of Sea Demons

Leviathan - class, the strongest kind, capable of destroying an entire country if left alone.

Whale - class, they serve as tanks, taking all the hits for other sea demons. They are mainly the ones analyzing, adapting, then spreading the info.

Shark - class, an attack force, they hide behind the Whale - class SD's if they feel they are about to die.

Dolphin - class, they serve an information relayers, once the Whale - class SD's analyzes and adapts, they take that information and spread it, either by direct contact using their tendrils, or by cutting off a piece of themselves and sending it to other sea demons, their main priority is reaching either the Central Nervous System (CNS) or a checkpoint.
-
The Central Nervous System, it is like its name, the brain of all Sea Demons, so long as the CNS exists, so will the Sea Demons. 
-
Checkpoints, they have a hotline to have direct contact with the CNS to spread the information for their species to evolve. 
-
Sea Demons, because of their extremely adaptive nature, have extremely strong neurons, strong enough to produce energy, that energy can then be used to produce electricity.
