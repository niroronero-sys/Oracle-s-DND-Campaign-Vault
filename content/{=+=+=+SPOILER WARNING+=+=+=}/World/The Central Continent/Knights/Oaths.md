
Lady to Knight Oath

| An oath that bounds a Knight to a Lady, their lady must be protected at all costs, if their lady dies, so will the knight.
| The world will not recognize the oath unless the knight kneels before their lady, the lady gives her hand with True Love in their hearts, and the knight shall recite this exact passage of words.

"The knight of my lady until my final breath, I offer my life in exchange for your favor, it is by my love, for yours. I will have my death in your devotion."


Dueler's Oath

| An oath that mutually assures the two parties participating in combat know the consequences and weight a duel will bring.
| The world will not recognize the duel unless this exact passage of words is said.

"Heed no voice but yours and mine. You and I are fully aware of the ramifications, whatever the intention, whatever the motive, this is an oath made in blood, for blood. Show me your courage, show me your will, show me your honor, then die on that hill."


Inheritance Oath

| An oath that is an exchange between Title holder and inheritor.
| Before engaging in a life taking duel, the inheritor must bear a striking resemblance to the Title holder both physically and mentally.

For the Title holder:
"Mine self is thine, we cannot go back after crossing this line: take up your arms and claim my sight, see the world as I do. Harden your heart and pierce mine, you shall leave no residue. "

For the Inheritor:
"Thyself is mine, I may perish if I rescind a step. discard your worries as from now, we have never met. Thy name shall cross the river to be forgotten; thy title shall live. "
