Odd-job manifest.
The Commissioner's Guild is a pillar guild in the Central Continent, not by being a strong raid guild, but by being an orderly quest giver and receiver. 

Anyone can post a quest on the guild's tabs as long as they pay the 5% reward commission fee. 

Wherein if you post a commission, 5% of the reward will go to the guild as a posting fee.

---
The Guild Classifies its freelancers via a star system going as high as 7-stars. 

As obvious it may be, you are only being compensated by commissions.

The star system helps reduce the risk of new expediters taking on requests that go way beyond their experience. 

Every expediter starts out as a 1-3 star. The jobs they can accept go from odd-jobs to simple gate guarding, they are eligible for the mentorship program wherein they will be assigned to a 6-Stars expediter(7-Stars on some occasions) for them to observe or be trained.

From 4-Stars onwards, they are able to create their own party's to accept commissions as a group.

The advantage of being in a party is that if the job was failed or worsened, the responsibility of it is shared instead of it just being on one person, ergo, it will be on their track record.

---
There have only been a few 7-Stars that we know of, most of them are elusive.

Blue Otter, is a 7-Star Expediter who is a popular choice by the guild officials to be used as a mentor and be assigned 1-3 stars for them to be trained. Safe to say that ever since Blue Otter has been appointed as a 7-Star Expediter, the mortality rate has significantly lowered, the mentorship program is truly a life-saver in times like these. Blue Otter's hallmark is their fluffy brown boots that barely makes a sound.

Periwinkle Hare, is an eccentric 7-Star Expediter who is extremely popular with the ladies. They say it's much better to mind your own business than for her to find entertainment in messing with you. She is often found travelling with a knight. Some reports say that Periwinkle Hare is actually a 'he', though such information has yet to be verified. Periwinkle Hare's hallmark is her purple eyes, though reports say that she always keep her eyes closed, akin to a fox.

Green Elk, is an incredibly dangerous 7-Star Expediter, please do not approach unless necessary, she possesses an incredible power to tame any living creature to follow her every command, the details of how are still unclear, she has said verbatim "Rules of Beasts, animals like such always follow the orders of something vastly stronger than them." Green Elk's hallmark is the elk skull she wears for a helmet, note that the horns of the skull are massive and incredibly ornate, with a lot of talismans and other such decorations hanging from it.

Black Vulture, whatever you do, do not accept anything from him. Black Vulture's hallmark is their massive black coat.

White Hummingbird, we're still unsure if there is a 7-Star Expediter titles "White Hummingbird"

---

For now, there are only 4 confirmed 7-Star Expediters, whether there are more will depend, some like to show off their badges, some hide it.
