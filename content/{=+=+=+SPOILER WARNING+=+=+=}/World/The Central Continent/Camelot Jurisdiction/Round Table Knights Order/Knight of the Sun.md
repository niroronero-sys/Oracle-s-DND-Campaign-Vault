Gawain.
He wields a greatsword, heavy and in that sword's core is the symbol of a sun placed as a rune core, it absorbs the sun rays and channels it into the sword, Hes loud and bright, blonde hair red eyes, long gold lashes and a bright smile.

He is a hearty man with the strength of an ox.

He is strongest when using his greatsword, channeling the unmatched might of the sun into his blade. When he meets an enemy he respects, he stabs his sword into the ground and readies his fists, in his own words "COME! SHOW ME WHAT KIND OF MAN YOU ARE!" 

A captain leading multiple apprentices, leading expeditions into the great forest but being careful to not go too deep, there are unexplained monsters within that forest, and they only get stranger the close you get to the Faerie King's Domain.

-+Tender is the Night+-

The moonlight glimmers upon the knight's blade on his back, an apprentice stores his telescope into his bag after seeing the moon reveal a derelict church in the middle of the woods, it's stone walls overrun by the greenery.

The old wooden doors groan as they push it open, the light of the moon passes through the ornamented windows, the smell of iron and rot is suffocating, a red substance drips from the podium, then they see two dark figures above it, one feasting upon the other, blood dripping from its victim's neck and from its mouth.

The knight signals his subordinates to stand by, he keeps his right hand hovering about the hilt of his sword.
"Step into the moonlight and illuminate yourself!"

The creature unhands its victim and wipes the blood off of its cheek, then the solid steps echoing through the church and it steps into the moonlight, a pale complexion almost like corpse, crimson eyes and an unnatural charm, tell-tale signs of a creature of the night.

The creature's eyes wander and look through their equipment and lands on the knight's hand hovering on the hilt of a weapon.
"Humor me, what brings thee to this forsakened house of the lord?"

The knight pulls out his sword and stabs it into the ground, his eyes announces that hes seen through a ruse, then he looks for a reflection of the creature in the steel of his blade, zilch.

The knight readies his fists within his gauntlets with a battle-ready smile on his face.
"I am Sir Gawain of Camelot, Knight of the Sun. Now Identify yourself, creature of the night."

The creature of the night sees the core on the knight's weapon and he grins.
"Very well."

Bats spiral around the creature as he announces himself
"I am Helix Adelaide Pandemonium, second heir to the Pandemonium family, I welcome your duel, Knight of the Sun."
