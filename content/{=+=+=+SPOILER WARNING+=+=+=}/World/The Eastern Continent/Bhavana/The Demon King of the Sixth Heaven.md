"Vijeta Vineshakaj."

The tyrant who swallowed two surrounding kingdoms simply because she can.

"Submit or die."

Vijeta Vineshakaj's terrifying ability comes from her ability to make replications of herself and even what she's using.

If she is holding a gun and she replicated herself a thousand times, then she would have a thousand soldiers with deadly aim and one mind. Note that the only limit to how much she can replicate is what she can handle, so far, we've only seen her replicate herself a thousand times. 

Her military prowess comes not from strategic battles, but pure scorched earth. In the battle of river samskrta between Kingdom Pradoshah and Empire Bhavana which is ruled by Vijeta Vineshakaj.

Report from a soldier who surrendered was
"Those... shells... We couldn't tell what was real and what wasn't... I..I-I saw an artillery shell land and explode in front of me, and yet nothing happened to us. Others... they saw what happened, they thought that the sustained artillery bombardment was nothing more than a bluff... we learned the hard way that that wasn't the case..."
