+How can you see me with those eyes?+

The lone, vile, disgusting, deplorable, heartless tyrant walks through the pseudo-graveyard of her own making

She steps on a body, then tramples it. The sound of flesh expulsing blood and bone crushed under her boots fill the silent field.

She holds an apple as she slowly makes her way forwards. 

She looks up, sees the stubborn golden sun beams peeking through the smoky sky.

A gaze of contempt is aimed at the sun.

Her fingers dig into the flesh of the apple, its juice going down her hand. 

Then she crushed the apple, leaving it nothing but a core.

+Why did you look at me with those eyes?+
