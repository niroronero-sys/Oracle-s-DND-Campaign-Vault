==|Description==

Sigillum is a relatively short, pale-skinned young boy. Short, straight black hair. Only 
wears simple clothes, discarded rags or even sacks due to not being able to afford better 
clothing.

==| Backstory==

"Discard of it." 

Said Sigillum's father, Archmage Laqueus, to his concubine, who cradled in her arms
yet another child.

"But-"

"No. I will have none of it."

With the mere snap of his finger, she was frozen entirely in place. No movement.
Not a breath, not a blink, not even the tiniest twitch.

Laqueus did not spare the child any remorse or even an acknowledging glance. 

He turned his back on the now still concubine, and immediately ordered his nearby servants
to take the child and rid of it. And to another, he ordered that they get another concubine 
immediately.

You see, the Royal family of the Cincinnos bore ideologies similar to just about 
any other. They believed in complete and total militaristic power as well as the purity
of blood. So in order to align with this militaristic belief, Laqueus forbade himself 
and anyone in his lineage to reproduce with non-magical blood to produce stronger offspring.

There were successful attempts, yes, but he thought it was all progressing all so slowly. He was beginning to go mad. 
Eventually, out of frustration, Laqueus drank himself silly and demanded that they get yet another concubine.
Because of his lousiness, he did not sense the absence of magical power from the concubine. She soon birthed a son for 
Laqueus.

Despite being sloppily thrown out, Sigillum, by some miracle, perservered. He was found by a humble librarian doing their rounds.
With barely any hesitation, his new mother picked him up from the pile of discarded things and cradled him in her arms.
She was the one to name him Sigillum. She raised him for 15 years, teaching him how to read and write. 
Sigillum grew fond of reading and studying. His mother soon learned that her adoptive son had magical power. 
She had never been prouder to see a fallen book frozen with it's pages splayed open midair. 
She moved on to teaching him how to use his magical power, but as she did, she learned something else. It was the nature
of his magic, and the shocking news that he cannot learn any other sort of magical affinity or element outside of his own.
After learning what his magic truly was, his mother kept training him.

The Cincinnos bore a magical technique special only to them. Their spells allow them to grant a "limiter" onto matter, concept,
energy, and even magic. They could completely stop a cannonball fired at them at alarming speeds. They could temporarily
seal away an opponent's magic, energy, or strength. They can command mere molecules by "limiting" their capacity to move.
True mastery of this technique grants great precision. 
At the highest level of this technique stands Archmage Laqueus, 
who mastered the strongest technique available to them: The ability to freeze even time. 

In exchange for this reality breaking power, they are unable to learn any other spells.

Another year has passed and Sigillum begins to find interest in adventuring. Despite her wishes, she wants the best for Sigillum, 
and if he thinks this is what he wants to do, then so be it. After reluctantly freeing Sigillum from her crushing embrace, 
she bids him farewell with tears in her eyes. Sigillum then applies to become an adventurer, which, despite his age, was still somehow able 
to apply.

He constantly brings around the first book his mother had given him as way to remember her.

At his current level, Sigillum can stop matter for indefinitely. As for magic on living beings, he is slightly more limited, only
being able to do so temporarily. This factor is also dependent on the size.

[specific skill/ability]

Sigillum can cast two spells out of the four
the Cincinno bloodline can. 

Spell 1: Locke
This is the spell that allows Sigillum to apply a limitation to physical matter and beings.

Locke’s duration is dependent on the nature of the subject that it is being cast on.

There are two main factors:

The size, weight, and whether or not it is biological.

Sigillum is capable of indefinitely stopping objects with the size and mass equal to that of 2 buses.

For anything larger, he can stop for 5-10 minutes, shorter for when the object is larger/heavier, longer for when it is smaller.

If Sigillum casts this spell on any biological creature, it is automatically temporary.

For beings about the size of a human or slightly taller, Sigillum can freeze them for a whole day. This is if Sigillum decides to freeze them as a whole, instead of a single part.

For anything larger, he can only freeze them for 5 minutes maximum.

Spell 2: Seele

This is the spell that allows Sigillum to manipulate energy and other such things intangible such as magic and concepts.

The duration is dependent on the sheer power of the energy he is manipulating.

So say, for instance, Sigillum manipulates the kinetic energy of a falling anvil and limits its ability to accelerate downward. He now has to hold the spell against the force of gravity, which is trying to give it downward velocity. In this case, the anvil stays up much shorter than if he just used Locke, because he directly manipulates the molecules and not the energy. The duration in this case is only 12 hours maximum.

If casted on a person or any such being,
he can temporarily inhibit the receiver from using their mana to channel into a spell, for again, 5 minutes at maximum, regardless of size. Really only used as a failsafe if the target is too large for Sigillum to use Locke.


As for the two spells that he cannot cast, these are:
Locke: Ultimate.
Seele: Ultimate.

Only Archmage Laqueus has been able to cast these.

limitations posed by Locke. But, the user expends a great deal of mana and has to cast a magic circle and maintain it for as long as can. The second the circle goes down, the receiver or the object is freed.
