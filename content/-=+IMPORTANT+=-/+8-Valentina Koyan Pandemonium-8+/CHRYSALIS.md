Clathrus archeri, or its common name: the Octopus Stinkhorn.

In an extremely rare event— or rather, under specific circumstances, a chrysalis is formed within the heart of the plant; within that chrysalis lies an embryo for a "vampire" 

Under the guise of a garden, the fungi receives moonlight and with it: the embryo matured- a living humanoid organism emerges from it. 

Born without a name, neither a purpose, these children wander the dense forest in search for sustenance. 

A few of them found venison— then proceeded to drain the life out of it, they gather around the prey, droplets of blood leaving their mouth as they finish their feast. 

One chrysalis though emerged much later than all the others, there were 20 of them in total; she was the 20th to be born. 

As the light of the moon reaches her face, she looked down to see where she was born; she began consuming it— she bled, she bled from her orifices while consuming it, but her blood never touched the ground, some of it enveloped her face, filtering what she breathes.

Her blood soon encases her silhouette, a layer separating her from everything.

She looks up at the waning crescent moon, its glow ingested by her iris.


---

She strolls through the forest until she finds the light of the moon permeating the leaves westbound.
