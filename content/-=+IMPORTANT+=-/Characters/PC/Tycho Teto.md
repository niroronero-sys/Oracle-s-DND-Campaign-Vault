|==Description==

Sharp-eyed gunslinger with tousled crimson hair, His coat is tailored clean, and meticulously kept, every buckle and strap serving a purpose. matched with the twin pistols at his hips. Clad in dark, militaristic leathers marked with symbols of the strict code he follows, he upholds his own rigid sense of order. No matter who fell beneath it.

Backstory: Formed within a lab of an oppressive nation, his mother was a researcher who used her own DNA to infuse it within a homunculi and form Tycho, he was being tested for his capabilities every single day, they mixed his genetics with those of an eagle's, making him have eyes a thousand times sharper than an eagle and senses sharper than a newly forged blade. His first field mission was an extremely important one, to infiltrate the D.A.M's gates and capture a Sea Demon. He succeeded. Though while the researchers were testing and analyzing the Sea Demon in the lab, an accident happened— An accident that caused the escape of every single test subject within the lab, Vespire, a fellow homunculi like him escaped the lab with him. In their darkest moments, They encountered a seasoned bounty hunter, a rogue named Teto who understood the corrupt nature of the nobility and had dedicated his life to bringing them down. Drawn to this man's charisma and skill, They pleaded for mentorship. The bounty hunter saw potential in Them, a fierce determination, and the spark of rebellion hidden beneath their pain. Under his guidance, Tycho learned the art of gunplay, honing his marksmanship and developing a keen tactical mind. As he trained, Tycho embraced a new ideology. He began to formulate his own code of justice, one that placed order and discipline above morality. Each target became a means to an end; every crime he committed was justified under the guise of his rigid rules. Armed with a pair of meticulously crafted twin pistols and draped in dark, militaristic leathers adorned with symbols of his personal code,
he became a ruthless gunslinger, feared by the corrupt and respected by those who sought his assistance. No longer the helpless boy yearning for justice, Tycho transformed into a calculated enforcer of his version of order. He would dismantle the power structures that had wronged him, navigating the shadows of the city with an unyielding resolve. However, Tycho maintained a clear distinction between himself and the unscrupulous nobles he hunted. In his mind, he was not a monster; he was an agent of a greater truth, delivering a harsh but necessary justice to those who abused their power. He understood that to achieve true order, one must sometimes embrace chaos, and he was unafraid to wield that chaos as a weapon to reshape the world to fit his vision.
