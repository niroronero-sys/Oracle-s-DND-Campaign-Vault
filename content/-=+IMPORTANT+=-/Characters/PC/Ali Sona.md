Ali Sona/Mr Door wears a long velvet coat gown colored in deep midnight black and a capelet layered over the shoulders in blue and black; underneath all of that was a white blouse. |Backstory
Ali Sona/Door was a Sequence 1 of the Door pathway, angel level; he needed to reach Sequence 0 as he already had all neighboring pathways characteristics to ascend and to combat the outer gods that threaten to corrupt him as he was exiled and was essentially trapped out the cosmos in a desperate attempt to ascend he ran into the one of those said outer gods as he narrowly escaped using his authority to teleport around he found himself eventually sucked in a mysterious hole before inevitably pasing out. As he woke up, he found himself in a grassy field; his clothing was messy, and he was in a haze. Eventually, he realized that all his authorities, the sequences, and his powers were inactive and completely dormant. Beside him was a pair of gauntlets, and from there he ventured out.
