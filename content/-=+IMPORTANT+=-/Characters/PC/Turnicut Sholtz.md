==| Description==

Turnicut is a 4ft tall dwarf with a great waraxe that can cut through anything he needs to if he swings hard enough, he is covered in armor forged by his clan members with unknown material that can withstand great hits and magic to a certain degree. He enjoys quiet time, food, tea, and spending time with his companions in the adventurer guild.


==| Backstory==

Turnicut is a well known adventurer in the capital for his achivement for slaying orcs for days one end, claiming territories and dungeons in only a matter of weeks, and slaying a dragon in a single day, however he seeks to enjoy a simple life with peace and serenity to come. If the time comes when he has to carry his greataxe to fight for his freedom, then he shall swing and fight until his will is no longer.
