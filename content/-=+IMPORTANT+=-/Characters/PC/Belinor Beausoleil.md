Backstory:

Belinor Beausoleil  was adopted at the age of 10 and raised where she would spend the rest of her childhood and teenagehood in the region of Provence, France. However, she later moved to an urban area to study her academics.

  

She is studying in an academy where she’s currently pursuing her interest in Fashion. She is very well-known at the Academy as a dedicated, caring figure to all of her peers and professors. Especially, during her earliest years, she excelled in all of the subjects, and participated in competitions, always part of the Top 3 consistently. She was also reliable and just generally a great person to others and doesn’t have any scandals/horrid rumors about her. It was like the closest definition of a “perfectionist”. However, just like everyone else, she has her own secrets and flaws. 

  

When she was young, she got in a tragic “accident” where her eye got badly scarred and visually impaired by someone unknown and unrecognizable to her. She remembered the suffering to endure the pain of her unfortunate, strange scar that felt like she’s on a brink of death that words can’t explain alone. When it healed, she was very self-conscious and insecure about it until now of how “inhumane” and “severe” it was. Her guardian felt pity and sadness about it, so she gave her a temporary solution. Every Belinor’s birthday, this guardian of Belinor would give her  a sacred box filled with 12 potions/elixirs  that would sustain her for a year as a present. This magical potion can alter its physical appearance to the person’s desire and “heal” their pain which can last approximately a month. Its special ingredients came from an enchanting garden where her guardian only has the knowledge where the location is. She will also soon embark on a journey to discover  that will eventually connect to the events that led to that “accident.
