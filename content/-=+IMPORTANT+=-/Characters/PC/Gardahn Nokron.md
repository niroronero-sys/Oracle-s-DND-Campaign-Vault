A dragonkin born in a city deeply rooted in corruption, he was trained, his body hard as steel, he was taught the ways of the blade and his race arts. But it wasn't all sunshine and butterflies; his parents died due to an added rule, “No dragonkin” (isn't that unique). He fled the city into the forest; he had nothing as guards chased him 
He hid in bushes, and as guards left, he heard how they treated captured dragonkin; some were turned into slaves, and some were used as trophies. As he escaped the city, he had to fend for himself. He started a new life in the north, where nobody would expect a dragon to be. The snow didn't affect his thick scales; his skills were enough to hunt for food. He lived a quiet village life. He trained to perfect the only skills he was taught. He never trained without a reason; he saw what happened under the curtains, which fueled his hatred against the law, and saw that he could make a “Paradise under his rule”. He wants to rule a city with his own laws (ifykyk). And he wanted to be known to have an impact on this world; he aspires to be a Knight. He know roams the world to achieve the goals he set, knowing he has the skills and talents to persevere and move past obstacles.
