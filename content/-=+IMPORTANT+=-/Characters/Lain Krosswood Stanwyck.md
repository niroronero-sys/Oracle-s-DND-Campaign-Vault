==|Backstory==

"HOLD STEADY!" He ordered, yet the roaring screams of bombardment shells overshadowed his voice. Their position, Yorktown was nothing but a bomb zone. The attempt of evacuation from Cornwallis was long gone when a squall arrived, An opportunity to escape has been shortened to be Imperceptible. It was one hell of a morning during October 16, 1781. Sir Stanwyck has watched his men lose their morale, the constant barrage of shells has made them almost deaf, while some jerk to the slightest of a sound. "Is there nothing we could actually do?" Stanwyck muttered, his confidence wavering. If he'll ever see another day, past the bombardments, the fear and trauma in his people, and the possibility of seeing his home- **Thud.** He fell, the deafening sounds of war stopped. The shouts of one of his men in pain as they lose a part of themselves, the frantics of reloading the same musket over and over again, the heavy footsteps everytime they reposition, the smoke that covers half of the battlefield, Gone. Stanwyck lays bare in an place he doesn't recognize yet it feels.. safe, nostalgic even.. On his hands, on his body, he feels a patch of grass that he thought he may never touch again. He stares directly at the sky, not covered in smoke, not in fog, but it was clear. He hears the birds chirping, the trees rustling, and the gust of wind. Nothing else. He slowly sits up, finding himself in a area he doesn't recognize. The war in his head was still there, but it feels all too real, like it was in his imagination. So much runs into his head. "What is this?" "Where could I be?" "Was it all nothing but my imagination?" Can't be, he looks at himself, he's still wearing the 17th regiment of foot uniform. "Am I dead?" He questions, he looks down, up, left, and right. This place doesn't seem like heaven nor hell.. He stood up slowly, boots crunching on the tall grass. He takes his first step into this foreign land, moving forward without a proper sense of direction, just yet to explore. There was stonewalls built so high it could rival the palace of Westminster. Stanwyck thinks this place is not so bad, being alone might be the only problem he would have to deal with, but he has a feeling that something is watching over him. Not so long after, he finds a entrance to a cave, It has glowing crystals, gems, and ores he physically can't take with his bare hands. Curiosity takes over and he enters the said cave. It's small, yet the glowing ores and crystals make it feel more significant. Stanwyck analyzes each one with a depth of curiosity, he haven't seen a crystal before, but has heard of it. Staring at each one, he soon finds one that seems out of place, it's black, almost liquid, like it was splattered onto the wall and left there. He walks up to it, seeing how the liquid seems to move on it's own randomly, Radiating a sense of a unnamed strange object or a organism. Before he could step back, some of it Lashes out and reaches for his arm. He stumbles and steps back, but it had already clinged into him, more of the liquid began to latch on the more he struggles and resists, his hand up to his face was covered in this mysterious liquid, After a tug and pull, it stopped, half of it lunged to his face, and mostly turned his top overall black, and his heart becomes fully exposed yet covered in liquid and a set of bones like a cage around it. He's confused and still trying to get rid of it, but it already started to feel like his skin. His other eye that was sealed shut, began to open. It sees things more differently, yet he retains full control of it. Out of panic he runs out the cave. He runs away as further as he could from the cave, after thinking he's far enough, he notices he wasn't out of breathe or a sigh of exhaustion, Almost as if the liquid that latched onto him changed his internal body and outside appearance. However, the lingering sensation of something watching over him didn't go away, it's loud enough in his head that it's the only thing he could think of. Turning around he immediately saw a figure, a lean, greek-physique build. Yet their skin is gray, and the noticeable details on their head is one eye. It holds a weapon, an knife, it blinks before suddenly walking towards him with the knife prepared to stab him. Stanwyck tries not to panic, yet the uncertainty of what this being is, what's it's capable off is bothering him in this life or death situation. Fortunately, He killed the almost human being/cyclops with the same weapon it held. He stood back as he watches the body stays motionless with the knife embedded onto it, their blood is different, colored pink. Judging from this.. This place is no longer safe. The term death still exists in this place. Fast forward, He stopped counting how many he has killed, he studied and experimented with this beings, all of them can't talk, they still behave like humans, but they have the intent to kill him on sight, all of them have weapons in the next area he goes into, and they adapt. He fought a different being, it was able to cast flames on their sword. It was more dangerous than the others. Yet, he was able to kill it and take It's weapon, He woke up from his sleep, tired and exhausted, he looked back at the being he killed earlier, the corpse is still there, or the ash it remained. But something's new, behind the corpse was a glowing blue oval shaped light, constantly shaking or moving. His heart pounds, not sure what it is, but on the other end, it shows a place. Maybe a place different from here, it was a gamble, it could be more dangerous than the area he's in. But he made a choice, He strapped the weapons he scavenged, including the weapon he took from the creature he killed earlier, and stepped in. A white blinding light flashes against his eyes, forcing him to close it and cover them.. Soon it went away, he slowly opened it and found himself standing infront of a infanstracture, it looks different, then someone bumped into him, not the same creatures with one eyes, but actual humans. "Watch where ye going." The bearded man says before continuing his walk, Stanwyck looks around and find it to be actually a city. With humans, actual humans.. and some species that has a distinct body structure of human anatomy.. He looks back at the building, it says the words TAVERN.. He's relief and so glad that he's finally gone in that hellhole, He takes it upon himself to maybe walk inside the TAVERN to relax.. He finds a stool, reflecting upon what he had gone through, the INN was comforted with other people inside, he analyzes and sees how they are wearing clothes.. He sees a warrior, a nun, an warlock.. all of those he never seen before, This place was different aswell.. But it seems safer, let alone have people he could finally communicate, It was so long since he was able to talk to a person properly.. He hears the door creak as someone enters, someone who looks too young to be in such a place for drinking.. He looks like he knows his way around, maybe Lain Krosswood Stanwyck from the 17TH Regiment Of Foot Officer could ask for a sense of directions or a need of assistance..
