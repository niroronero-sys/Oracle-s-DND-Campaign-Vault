==|Description==

White hair, Ivory skin and white Irises and she’s 3 '4 tall with a nice personality being a kind, gentle, and pure fairy helping those who need her assistance. 
She looks like a young beautiful maiden with fairy wings that makes her more special. 

Backstory: When Ivory was a very young fairy, she lived with her clan peacefully but Ivory wanted to explore and meet new people and she was given permission to venture the world but in a condition that she must collect artifacts, anything that she found interesting and return to tell her clan about her experiences when she explored the world. 
Her clan gave her protection charms and said their goodbyes. Over the years She misses her clan and decided to return to her clan but when she arrived she only saw her once lively village turned into a gutted ruin, only the shell of her once peaceful village remained as she saw rotting corpses of her own companions and family with a mysterious black substance all over their body and the damages was beyond her abilities to fix. She decides to bring justice to whoever did this to her clan and so she went out to seek clues and find answers while she visits her now destroyed village for clues and gives respect for those who died. Even all of that happened to her she’s still a nice and gentle fairy.
