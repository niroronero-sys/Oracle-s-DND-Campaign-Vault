Camelot is a nation that was founded by knights, ruled by knights, and relies on knights. But not every knight is in Camelot.

Camelot is a court, a neutral party in the center of the continent, having knights from every other nation, it serves as a peacekeeper, or an enforcer of law. Wars also have laws.

Knights can be either part of a Knight order or a pilgrim, but fact is, every true knight has gone on a pilgrimage and has been knighted by the Knight of Genesis.

Camelot Jurisdiction was founded 230 years ago, and the current Roundtable Knight Order is made up of the 5th generation of Roundtable Knights.

District 1, King of Knights, Arthur's district, a lavish district with many skyscrapers and large arenas for knightsports, it requires an expensive lifestyle to be here.

District 2, Knight of Will, Galahad's district, a large educational facility. A safehaven for the innocent. The Executioner Knights' head quarters is in this district.

District 3, Knight of Treason, Mordred's district, where there is a strict focus on law and order

District 4, Knight of the Sun, Gawain's district, where KnightSports is only 2nd to District 1

District 5, Knight of Perception, Tristan's district, where architecture and engineering is the main focus. 

District 6, Knight of Loyalty, Bedivere's district, the culture here seem to love the arts, infidelity is taken very seriously here.

District 7, Knight of Justice, Percival's district, has heavy parallels with district 3, though KnightSports is a heavy focus here, 3rd only to district 4.

District 8-12, Unoccupied, currently under Mordred's rule.
