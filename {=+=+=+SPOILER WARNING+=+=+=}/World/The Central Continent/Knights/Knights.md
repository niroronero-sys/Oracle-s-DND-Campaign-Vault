Knights are people who uphold a belief, person, glory, honor, name, or title.

They are people who have something to protect.

Knights are always bound by [[The Code of Chivalry]] and by [[Oaths]]

Each knight is born with the potential of awakening a title, whether they are able to awaken the title or not is pre-determined. 

Titles can be inherited through the Inheritance Ceremony. 

Two titles of the same name cannot exist at the same time.
